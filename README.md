# ByaheGo

Rent the right ride, anywhere. A vehicle rental marketplace for Cebu City where renters book cars, vans and motorcycles, and local owners earn from their idle vehicles.
>
## About

ByaheGo ("byahe" means trip or journey in Cebuano/Filipino) connects people who need a ride with vehicle owners who want to earn from theirs. The platform has three sides:

- Renters search, compare and book vehicles, with pick-up at a partner hub or by delivery.
- Hosts list vehicles, set pricing and availability, and track bookings and earnings.
- Admins oversee bookings, verification and platform activity.
